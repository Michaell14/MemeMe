    Your friends, cut out

    Drag photos of friends onto the smiley in your menu bar. MemeMe cuts every person out as a die-cut sticker and, if you say yes, groups them by who they are. Pull Maya’s signature face and there’s Maya.

    Cutting out and matching happen on your Mac with Apple’s Vision. Faces are only compared with stickers you made yourself.

    Memes you already saved

    MemeMe watches Downloads, Desktop and your screenshots folder. A new meme is searchable about two seconds after it lands. It reads the text and the faces into a small index and leaves your files where they are. Personal photos and chat screenshots are skipped.

    Reaction GIFs

    GIFs from GIPHY fill in next to your own. Your face becomes one mood word on your Mac, and that word is all MemeMe sends to GIPHY. Popular moods are cached for a day, so most GIFs show up instantly.

    Tiny, quiet, local

    MemeMe is Swift on Apple’s own frameworks. There’s no account, no bundled machine-learning model and no third-party code. The camera runs only while the panel is open, and each frame is read on your Mac and dropped.

    It keeps out of your way too. Indexing runs at low priority, two images at a time, and pauses in Low Power Mode or when your Mac runs hot.

    1. 1

      Open the disk image

      Open MemeMe.dmg and drag MemeMe onto the Applications folder next to it.

    2. 2

      Open it the first time

      MemeMe isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it once. On macOS 15 or later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14, Control-click MemeMe, choose Open, then click Open again.

    3. 3

      Say yes to the camera

      The panel opens by itself the first time and asks for the camera. After that, look for the smiley in your menu bar, or press ⌃⌘M from anywhere.

    4. MemeMe keeps itself up to date. When a new version is out, a dot appears on the smiley in your menu bar and the panel offers it. One click installs it and restarts MemeMe. Afterwards macOS may ask once more for the camera and your folders.
    5. GIFs need a free GIPHY API key for now. Get one at developers.giphy.com, then choose Set GIPHY API Key… from the smiley menu. Your own memes and stickers work without it.
