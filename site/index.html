MemeMe

A small menu-bar app for Mac that finds the right reaction image. In any chat, press ⌥M, type a word or make a face, and press Return. The image is copied and you paste it.

How it works

Searching with your face

Press ⌘F in the panel and the camera turns on. Hold an expression for about a third of a second and it becomes the search. There is no shutter. MemeMe compares you with your own resting face, so small changes count: one raised brow, a wink, a tongue out.
