# MemeMe — product & interaction spec (v1)

MemeMe is a macOS 14+ menu-bar app. You press ⌃⌘M in any chat, make a face, and pick a reaction; it is copied and you are back in the chat. The design goal is **as little friction as possible**, and the app must stay **tiny and lightweight**.

The redesign's canvas (the drawer, the layouts tried for it and the Notch Black system) lives at https://claude.ai/artifact/6XruumvWCYSvhtqqNAoQp5, and the face editor's (live glow, editing, drawing, the editor's anatomy) at https://claude.ai/artifact/Co3mWPGgV9UZ3L4muq36tS (the board "A1 · For You tab" is this spec); the earlier screens at https://claude.ai/artifact/T9nxHbX2DHg5ChhWdT8Tqf. The panel is a **black drawer hanging from the notch**, with **two rows of 5** for the tab showing: **For You** (your things mixed with the classic memes on top, GIPHY's GIFs under them; the panel always opens on it), **GIFs**, **Stickers** or **Memes**.

## Principles

1. **Your face is the search box: passive to start, direct to refine.** Holding a face still starts the search, with no shutter, mode toggles or filters. What counts shows as a glow on your own face, and once a face is caught you can take hold of it: push a feature further, resize what counts, or draw on it (Editing your face, below). (Sending yourself, below, is a separate view with its own shutter and filters.)
2. **One commit.** ↵, a number key 1–9, or a click copies the result, closes the panel and leaves you in the chat. MemeMe never pastes or sends for you (no Accessibility permission, ever).
3. **Undo instead of confirm.** Automatic actions (keeping files, making stickers, filing friends) report what they did and offer Undo.
4. **Never move what the user is about to click.** Your memes never change under you while you look; GIPHY's results arrive in their own row and never reshuffle yours.
5. **Tiny.** Swift + Apple frameworks only, no third-party packages, only the hybrid matcher's two Core ML models (README "How matching works"), camera only while the panel is open, an index instead of copies of the user's files.

## The panel (the drawer)

- A black drawer centered on the notch, flush with the top of the screen and drawn over the menu bar (a non-activating panel at window level `mainMenu + 2`; the chat app stays the active app and keeps its draft and caret). 736 pt wide: 712 between its 12 pt shoulders (inverted corners flaring into the top edge of the screen), bottom corners 24; about 300 pt tall, and about 540 pt while the face editor is open (it grows down, so nothing above moves). On a screen without a notch it hangs from the middle of the menu bar the same way.
- **The ears.** The drawer's top band is the menu bar's height (`NSScreen.safeAreaInsets.top` on a Mac with a notch, else the menu bar's, else the system's menu bar thickness while it hides), split by the notch (its width from `auxiliaryTopLeftArea` and `auxiliaryTopRightArea`; the ears meet without one).
  - **Left ear, the query field** (26 pt tall, round, Raised 2, at most 300 pt): always a search field, so search is never hidden behind the face. A 22 pt magnifier, then, once the camera caught a face, its word as a token (20 pt capsule on Line: a face, figure or sparkles symbol, the word in 12 pt semibold, 45 % while a new expression is held toward replacing it; × goes back to your recent picks: "Not this face"), then what you type (13 pt), else the placeholder (muted): "Type to search, or make a face" before a face is caught (just "Type to search" without a camera), "Type to refine" after (typing keeps the face). Typing with a held face kept (the face token), the glyph is a filled face: clicking it drops the face ("Stop using your face in this search").
  - **Right ear, the tabs:** For You · GIFs · Stickers · Memes · ★ Saved (12 pt medium, muted, 7 pt padding; Saved is the star alone so five fit beside the notch; the one showing on a Raised 2 pill in Text white, the star filled). Click, or Tab / Shift-Tab. The panel opens on For You every time.
- **The body** (20 pt in from the sides, 12 pt under the ears): the mirror column (104 pt) and, 16 pt to its right, **two rows of 5 tiles** (104 pt, 8 pt apart, the rows 12 pt apart). **While you make a face** (the panel just opened, nothing caught yet, the camera live or starting) the mirror is **216 pt**, so the small changes it reads are easy to see, and the rows hold 4 tiles each under 11 pt semibold muted titles: "Recent in Messages" (the app the panel opened over; "Recent", "Your newest" or "Classic memes" when that fits better) and "Frequent" (else "From GIPHY"). Once a face is caught the mirror shrinks back to 104 pt and the five-wide rows take over, before you pick, so nothing moves under the pointer while you choose. Under the tiles, right-aligned, "Powered by" and GIPHY's logo while GIPHY's GIFs show; the line keeps its height otherwise, so switching tabs never resizes the drawer. A footer below only while there is something to act on.
  - **Mirror column:** the live camera, mirrored, in a 104 pt circle (Raised behind it) with a 3 pt ring 2 pt out: Raised 2 at rest; while an expression or pose is held it fills clockwise from the top in Text white (a hold that is lost winds it back); when a pose becomes the search it flashes in the signal color and fades. Over the camera, the face's brows, eyes and mouth glow by how much each counts in the search showing: rounded outlines around Vision's regions in Text white, brighter and fuller the more the part counts (stroke 25 % + 15 % per weight, fill 4 % + 2.5 %), dashed Text 2 when it is ignored; pointing at the mirror lifts them a little. While live, a 26 pt camera button (Raised 2, black edge) on its bottom right opens Send yourself, and once a face is caught a 26 pt edit button (sliders) on its bottom left opens the face editor, as does a click on the mirror. Under it, centered: what the word beside the notch was read from (a face, figure or sparkles symbol, then "your face", "your pose" or "face and pose", 11 pt muted) and, under that, what counts most ("mostly your brows", "your whole face", "only your mouth", "mostly your mouth · ignoring your brows"; 11 pt medium, Text), else the hint (title 12 pt semibold, subtitle 11 pt muted, optional capsule action). "Find my memes" sits under it while you have no memes; "Look in Documents…" after a typed search that found fewer than 5 of yours, until Documents is watched.
  - **Drop:** while an image is dragged over the panel (not one of its own tiles) a dashed white card over the tiles says "Drop to search with it · It isn’t saved unless you keep it."
  - **Footer:** only while there is something to act on: memes being collected, the memes just added, or an update (see Library, Updates): a 1 px separator, then that one line. No key legend.
- **Tiles:** 104 pt, radius 10, on Raised: the thumbnail filling the tile (a sticker whole, cut out, 6 pt in; a classic meme whole, letterboxed). The focused tile has a 2 pt ring in the signal color 2 pt out and the ↩ chip at its bottom right (signal color, black ↩, 6 pt in): Return copies it. A GIF of yours carries a "GIF" tag at its bottom left (10 pt bold on 62 % black) where kinds mix, in For You's top row; in the Frequent row, how often you sent it ("14×") instead. One of your things you saved carries a filled star at its top right (20 pt circle, 62 % black); pointing at one of yours shows "☆ Save" (or "★ Saved") there instead, which toggles it, as do ⌘S on the focused tile and the right-click menu. GIPHY's GIFs and the classic memes have no star: they can't be kept. Only the focused tile animates. No number badges: 1–9 still pick, row by row. Empty slots keep their place (Raised; dimmer when nothing is on its way); a row with nothing in it says why ("No memes match “sad”.", "Your memes will show here.", "Make a face or a pose for GIFs from GIPHY.", "GIPHY isn’t answering right now.", "Nothing on GIPHY for this one.", "Drop a photo of a friend on the MemeMe icon to make a sticker.", "No stickers match.", "Turn on Web Discover in the menu for GIFs from GIPHY."). Right-click one of your things: More like this.
- No percentages or scope filters are shown in the search, and the tabs are its only mode. Weights show only as the glow on your face and, in the face editor, as its rings and a five-step meter per part.

### Recent

On open, before a face is captured, the grid shows **Recent in this app** (per frontmost app bundle id), newest first; tile 1 is the last item sent from this app, so ⌃⌘M then ↵ repeats a reaction. If there is no history, the newest own memes; if you have no memes, GIPHY's row shows GIPHY's "reaction" results (and the classic memes the most popular) so the first run is never empty. Under it, in For You, **Frequent**: your things sent at least twice, most sent first (ties: the latest sent), leaving out what the row above shows (`FrequentPicks`); without any, the row is GIPHY's as before. The × on the face token comes back here.

### Saved

The star saves one of your things to the Saved tab (newest first, kept in recents.json with what you sent; a deleted file leaves it). Only your own things: GIPHY's terms don't let its GIFs be kept, and the classic memes are fetched from Imgflip each time.

## Design system (Notch Black)

One black surface that matches the notch, one signal color, the Mac's own type (SF Pro). Every screen of the panel and its notices uses only these (`UI/Theme.swift`; the canvas's "Notch Black" board).

- **Color:** Ground `#000000` (every panel); Raised `#1C1C1E` (fields, empty tiles); Raised 2 `#2C2C2E` (tokens, the tab showing, hairlines); Line `#3A3A3C` (keycaps, outlines, dashed wells); Text `#F5F5F7`; Text 2 `#8E8E93` (everything else, 6.4:1 on black); Signal `#FF9A5C`, the app icon's orange, for "this one" or "done" only: the focus ring, the ↩ chip, the Copied check, the pose-caught flash, filled buttons.
- **Type:** 13 semibold (the query's word, titles, "Copied"), 13 regular (typed words, field text), 12 medium (tabs), 11 medium (keycaps, tags, captions, the GIPHY credit).
- **Shape and space:** radii 5 (keys, tags, the tab pill), 10 (tiles, fields), 24 (the drawer's bottom corners), round (tokens, the mirror), 12 inverted (the shoulders where the drawer meets the top of the screen); spacing on a 4-point grid, tiles 8 apart.
- **Rules:** orange means "this one" or "done", nothing else (the face's glow and the editor's rings are white); panels are black and hang from the top of the screen, nothing floats mid-screen; a type mark shows only where kinds mix; one key takes any pick (↩ the focused one, 1–9 any tile); settings stay in the menu-bar menu, never in the panel.

## Capturing the face (no shutter for the search)

- The camera starts on show and stops on hide (within 1 s). About 10 analyzed frames per second.
- **Peak-hold:** a face held for 0.3 s becomes the query (the sharpest/most intense frame from the last 0.5 s). Relaxing or talking does nothing.
- A **stronger version of the same expression** (≥ 15 % more intense, same direction) replaces the query after 0.3 s. A **different face** must be held for **0.6 s** (the ring around the mirror fills), a different gesture for 1 s. **Relax first** (a calm face for 0.4 s) and the next face or gesture takes only 0.3 s, like the first one (the relaxing counts until the next search starts); you never have to pull a bigger face to be heard. Talking never stays calm that long between words, so it still does nothing.
- **Face details.** Besides the overall expression, MemeMe reads the face's shape against your own resting face: the tongue out (a tongue-colored patch under the lower lip, the lip thickened), a wink (one eye shut, the other open), one raised eyebrow, pursed lips (duck face), a smirk (one mouth corner up) and an eye roll (eyes looking up). A clear one is an expression of its own and the search's word: "tongue out", "wink", "raised eyebrow", "duck face", "smirk", "eye roll", for your memes (those showing it, or named or tagged for it, come first), GIPHY and the classic memes. Added to a held face (a tongue out while smiling) it is a new face, held for 0.6 s. The face is measured in its own frame, turned level by the line through the eyes, so a tilted head doesn't read as an open mouth. Memes indexed by earlier versions have their face read again in the background.
- **The same word again turns the page.** When a new face (or a stronger version of the one held, or Space) gives the same word as the one showing (or a pose named in the same words), your memes are ranked for it again and GIPHY's and the classic memes show their next ones: the next GIFs of GIPHY's 24 (5 or 10, as many as the tab shows) and the mood's next 10 classic memes, wrapping around to the first. A new word starts both rows from the start.
- **Space** (with an empty search field) snaps the current face immediately, even a neutral one (deadpan, unimpressed).
- Feature weights are inferred: regions that moved most from the user's own resting face count most (1×–3×). They show as the glow on the mirror (above), and the face editor lets you set them (below). The resting face is learned silently from calm frames (about 20 numbers, never an image) and can be reset from the menu.
- **Pose:** your arms, elbows, hands and (standing back) hips count. A clear gesture counts for most of the match, finds memes with the same pose (mirrored ones too), and picks its own GIPHY and classic-meme word: both arms up "celebrate", hands at your temples "mind blown", hands on top of your head "oh no", a hand over your face "facepalm", hands together "clapping", a shrug, a T-pose "t-pose", a double-biceps "flex", a dab, hands on hips, arms crossed, one arm straight up "pick me", a hand raised beside your head with its palm open to the camera "hello" (with a fist, or a hand the camera can't read, the same arm has no word of its own and is described instead, see below), a bow, and the hand signs thumbs up, peace and pointing. Any other deliberate finger pattern (some fingers clearly straight, the others clearly curled: horns, hang loose, finger guns; not a thumb alone out of a fist, which a hand round a mug looks like too) is a hand sign too, so it starts a search with your arms hanging, though not while a hand rests in front of you (a phone or a mug at your chest, a hand on the mouse: their grips look like patterns). A plain face leaves the pose a small say. Hands on hips, arms crossed and a bow sit close to arms at rest, so they are held for 1 s; hands on hips and a bow need your hips in view, and without them arms crossed needs the wrists above the elbows (forearms folded on a desk aren't crossed). Arm shapes too weak to be a gesture (forearms on armrests) leave the word to the face. A pose held right after another becomes the search after 1 s when its word or its shape differs, or, without a word, its finger pattern (horns, then hang loose) (0.3 s when your arms came down and your face was calm for 0.4 s in between, except hands on hips, arms crossed and a bow); a missed frame or two (a joint lost, a far face flickering) doesn't break a hold. A hand covering your face still counts as being seen. Memes indexed by earlier versions have their pose read again in the background. No slider.
- **A pose with no word of its own** (one arm flexed beside your head, horns, a hand on your chin, arms out in front) is described in words on the Mac. Its joints become plain facts, never numbers and never left or right ("One arm: upper arm out to the side at shoulder height, elbow bent sharply, forearm up, hand beside the head." "The other arm hangs at the side." "Hands: not seen."), and MemeMe's rules make 1–3 GIF-tag words from them: "rock on", "hang loose", "finger guns" or "love you" for a finger pattern; "flexing"; "thinking" (a hand at the chin), "salute" (at the forehead), "hand on head", "hand on heart", "hands together"; "hands up", "raise hand", "arms out", "arm out", "bowing"; else "strike a pose". On macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence on, Apple's on-device model names the pose from the facts instead (text only; the rules' words when it has no answer or takes over 2 s), and the same pose held again gets the same name without asking. Those words are the search's word: the readout shows them (with sparkles when the model named the pose), GIPHY is searched for them, the classic memes row shows the templates they name and then the face's mood's, and your memes whose name, text or labels say them come a little higher. While the model answers, the GIPHY row shows placeholders and the classic memes stay as they are.
- **More like this** (right-click one of your things): uses it as the query. "Back to your face" (or Esc) returns.

## Editing your face (direct manipulation)

Holding a face is still how a search starts; the editor only refines a face that was caught, so the search is as fast as before (`FaceEditSession`, `FaceSketch`, `StrokeGesture`; the hi-fi canvas above).

- **Opening:** the edit button on the mirror, a click on the mirror, or ⌘E, once the camera's search shows a face (a pose alone has nothing to edit). The drawer grows down by the editor, under a 1 px Raised 2 hairline; the tiles above don't move. The mirror dims to 35 % and stops searching (a new face held meanwhile does nothing; Space does nothing); under it "Searching your edit" with "Back to your face". **Esc**, "Back to your face" or ⌘E again close the editor: the edits go and the face the camera caught is searched again. Typing, dropping an image, More like this or the capture view close it too.
- **The face** (200 pt, on Raised in a circle): the caught face drawn from its seven numbers, the way the search reads it: the head outline in Line, brows, eyes, pupils and mouth in Text white on black. Each number moves its feature a fixed distance per unit (brows 28 pt, eyelids 14, pupils 9, mouth corners 28 up and down and 30 out, lower lip 27, the head up to 45° of tilt), so a dragged feature follows the pointer.
  - **Round dots move a feature** (Adjust tool): the middle of each brow (or anywhere along it), each eyelid's top, the pupils (sideways: gaze), each mouth corner (up and down: smile; out and in: width), the middle of the lower lip (open), and a dot on a short stem above the head (tilt). Dots are 3.2 pt, black with a Text white ring; the one under the pointer or held grows to a white 4.6 pt dot in a 9 pt halo. While you drag, the part's starting shape stays under it as a dashed Text 2 outline, so you see how far you pushed it. The cursor says what you can do: an open hand over a dot, a closed one while dragging. Once a feature was dragged, the face detail the camera read (a wink, a tongue out) no longer counts, since the drawing can't show it: the word follows the seven numbers.
  - **Squares set how much a part counts:** each part has a ring, a capsule around it in Text white, 2 pt bigger on each side per step, its stroke and fill brighter per step (as on the mirror), dashed Text 2 at 0, where its features dim to 35 %. A 7 pt square on the ring's right edge (9 pt under the pointer) is dragged out and in: a step every 10 pt, 0 (ignored) to 5. A pinch on a part does the same (twice the size adds 5 steps). The parts start at the automatic weights, rounded (1–3).
  - **The pen** (Draw tool): a circle drawn around a part makes it count 5, a scribble over it (or a line through it) makes it 0, and a line drawn down from under an eye adds tears, which make the word "crying". A circle or scribble shows a moment and fades into the ring's change; tears stay. A stroke that is none of the three says "Circle a part, scribble one out, or draw a tear."
- **What counts** (beside the face): "What counts" (13 pt semibold), the summary as a sentence ("Mostly your mouth · ignoring your brows", 11 pt muted), then a row per part: its name (13 pt; muted when ignored), five 18 × 6 pt segments (Text white filled, Line empty, dashed when ignored), and − and + (24 pt circles, Raised 2; Raised and muted at the ends). This is also the way to the weights with the keyboard and VoiceOver.
- **Tools** (right): Adjust · Draw (a segmented control, 30 pt, Raised with the chosen segment Raised 2), a line saying what the tool does (11 pt muted), the last stroke's notice ("Tears drawn. Now searching “crying”."; 12 pt on Raised, with its own Undo when the stroke changed something, which the row below then leaves out), Reset (back to the face as it was caught; dimmed when nothing changed) and Undo (⌘Z; every change, Reset too), and the keys: esc back to your face, ⌘Z undo.
- **What follows:** every change re-ranks your memes at once with the edited face and its weights. The word beside the notch follows the edited face; when an edit changes it, the old word shows struck out before the new one for 2 s ("shocked → screaming"). GIPHY's row and the classic memes follow the word once you let go (a step, a stroke, Undo and Reset count as letting go), so GIPHY's hourly limit lasts.

## Send yourself (photo or GIF of you)

- The camera button on the mirror, or **⇧Space** with an empty search field (while the camera reads you), replaces the panel's contents with the capture view; **Esc** (or "Back to memes") returns. The search pauses meanwhile.
- **Capture view:** a 300 × 300 mirrored picture of you (radius 18, accent ring) with a round white shutter at its bottom; beside it "Send yourself", a status line and the filters as chips (4 columns); under both a separator and the legend "space or click: photo · hold: GIF · ← → filter · esc back".
- **Tap** the shutter (click, Space or ↵) for a photo (the picture flashes white). **Hold** it (longer than 0.35 s) to record a GIF: the ring fills in coral, the shutter turns into a stop square, and recording ends when you let go or after 3 s (up to 12.5 frames a second, 10 from a 30 fps camera, played at real speed; 320 px). Held past 3 s, the GIF is copied when you let go.
- The capture is exactly what the picture shows (mirrored, filtered), square: a photo is 480 px at the camera's VGA. It is copied like any pick (`reaction.png` as PNG data plus a file, or `reaction.gif` with GIF data), the panel closes, and the HUD says **"Copied your photo · ⌘V — not sent"** (or GIF) with a **Save as sticker** button for 5 s (longer while the pointer is over it). Captures are never kept otherwise.
- **Save as sticker:** your face (the photo, or the GIF's frame with the strongest expression) is cut out as a die-cut sticker and kept as one of your own memes ("You — happy", source "Camera"): it shows up in face searches and under "sticker" and "mine". The HUD confirms ("Saved “You — happy” as a sticker", with **Undo**) or says "Couldn’t find you in that one."
- **Filters** (← → or a click; None each time the view opens): the looks None, Noir, Comic, 8-bit, Deep fried; then, Snapchat-style on your face: Dog (floppy brown ears, a shiny black nose, and a tongue that hangs out while your mouth is open, longer the wider it opens), Cat (pointed ears, pink nose, whiskers), Bunny (tall ears, pink nose, buck teeth), Rainbow (a rainbow pours from your mouth while it's open, nothing while it's shut), Flowers (a crown of flowers), Heart eyes, Deal with it (pixel shades), Devil (red horns), Angel (a glowing halo, bobbing), Crown, Bug eyes, Clown (red nose, rosy cheeks), Mustache (a curly handlebar), Pig (ears and snout). Face filters follow your face about 10 times a second, turning with your head; drawn with Core Graphics, no bundled art. The search always reads the unfiltered camera.

## Typing, dropping, pasting

- Any letter types into the search field (it has focus when the panel opens). Digits never type: 1–9 always pick, the tiles in order, row by row (0 does nothing). Space types only once there is text.
- Typing stops the camera if it starts within 1 s of opening; otherwise the held face stays as a removable token "+ your face ×" that orders the text results ("+ your pose ×" when a clear gesture hid the face).
- Text search covers OCR text, Vision labels, file names, sources and friend names. Filters by word: "mine", "gif", "sticker", a friend's name. Own items, grouped by month (newest first). The GIPHY row follows the words once typing pauses for 0.6 s (at once when GIPHY answered them in the last 10 minutes; at least 3 letters or digits; while the panel asks “Is one of these Sam?”, nothing for a word that isn't ordinary meme vocabulary: a mood word, a mood's name or a classic meme's search word still goes): GIPHY's results for the typed words without filter words or your people's names (possessives too: "sam's"), in GIPHY's order and unfiltered. The classic memes row follows them at once (matched on the Mac). Clearing the words brings back the face's results (or the popular classic memes). If nothing matches and the word looks like a name, the panel asks **“Is one of these Sam?”** with up to 3 unnamed faces; clicking one names that person.
- If too few results, the last tile is "Not here? Look in Documents…" which adds a watched folder (one macOS prompt).
- **Drop or ⌘V an image onto the panel = search with it only** (never saved silently). "Keep it" saves it. ⌘V is treated as an image query only when the pasteboard holds an image that MemeMe did not put there itself.

## Committing

- ↵ (focused tile), 1–9, or click: copy, record as sent (per app), close the panel; the drawer folds back into the notch, which says so for 1.6 s: **“✓ Copied”** in its left ear (the check in the signal color, after a 20 pt thumbnail of the copy) and **“⌘V to paste”** (a keycap and muted words) in its right one. Other notices ("Copying…", "Saved “You — happy” as a sticker" with Undo) hang just under the notch in the same black shape.
- Within 300 ms after the last keystroke in the search field, ↵ is ignored (so ↵ meant to "run the search" doesn't commit).
- Drag a tile straight into the chat's message box: the clipboard is left alone; the panel closes on a successful drop.
- Clipboard formats: GIFs as a file (plus `com.compuserve.gif` data) so Discord and iMessage keep them animated; stills as PNG data plus a file. File names never leak names: own items keep their file name, everything else is `reaction.gif`, `reaction.png` or `sticker.png`.
- GIPHY GIFs: the full GIF (GIPHY "downsized", ≤ 2 MB) is downloaded ahead, into the HTTP cache only, for the row's first GIF and for any GIF that gets the focus, so copying is instant; otherwise it downloads on commit with the HUD showing "Copying…". It is written to a short-lived file only when it is copied or dragged; copy, drag and clipboard files are deleted within the hour (checked every 10 minutes), except the copy still on the clipboard.

## Closing

- Esc: first clears refinements (the face editor, text, more-like-this, image query); second closes.
- Any click outside the panel closes it (prevents ↵ meant for the panel from reaching the chat).
- ⌃⌘M toggles.

## Library that fills itself

- **First-run "You're set" card** (shown after the first pick on first run, replacing the top row): "Copied. Paste it with ⌘V." · "Next time, in any chat: ⌃ ⌘ M · Change" · checkbox "Open at login" (checked) · button "Find memes already on this Mac" with caption "Downloads, Desktop, Screenshots · stays on this Mac". It closes on the next app switch or Esc.
- If the card was skipped: a [Find my memes] capsule sits under the mirror while you have no memes; the menu has it too. No footer line asks.
- While indexing: footer "Finding your memes… 212 · newest first". After: one line on the next open "Added 312 from Downloads, Desktop · 1,140 skipped (photos, documents) · Review · Undo".
- Watched folders: Downloads, Desktop, and the screenshot location (from `com.apple.screencapture` → `location`, default Desktop). New files become searchable in about 2 s. Top level only. Images ≤ 25 MB: PNG, JPEG, GIF, HEIC, WebP, TIFF.
- A silent classifier sorts images: **meme** (in results), **maybe** (typed search only), **skipped** (personal photos, chat/DM screenshots; only reachable as a last resort in typed search under "From your photos"). Skipped images keep no face data or text.
- The index stores ~4 KB per meme (features + a base64 Float32 image fingerprint + OCR text). User files are never copied, except images explicitly handed in (dropped on the menu-bar icon), which go to `~/Library/Application Support/MemeMe/Kept`.
- Indexing runs at utility priority, at most 2 images at once, pauses in Low Power Mode or when the thermal state is serious, and can be paused for 1 hour from the menu.

## Friend stickers

- Subject lifting (Vision, macOS 14) cuts each eligible person out; a white die-cut outline is added; PNG ≤ 512 px stored in `Application Support/MemeMe/Stickers`.
- Each sticker is a normal library item (kind friend) with face features, so it shows up when you make a similar face. Friend stickers win ties but at most 2 appear in a face-search grid unless the query names the friend.
- Grouping (only after consent): a face embedding (Vision image feature print of the face crop) is compared against the user's earlier stickers only. ≥ 0.90 similarity → confident ✓; ≥ 0.80 → "Name?" confirm; else a new unnamed face. Naming is lazy (typed name → "Is one of these …?"). "Forget this person" exists in the menu via Library folder in v1 is out of scope; `forget(personID:)` exists in code.
- Note for the report: Vision's generic feature print is not an identity model; thresholds are conservative and the UI asks when unsure.

## Web discover (GIPHY)

GIPHY's results are shown the way GIPHY's rules ask: in their own row, in GIPHY's order, unfiltered, never mixed with your memes, never stored, under GIPHY's mark.

- On by default when a GIPHY API key is available (Info.plist `GiphyAPIKey` baked at build time from the `GIPHY_API_KEY` env var, or set from the menu). Without a key there is no GIPHY row and the menu item reads "Web Discover (GIPHY): No API Key", still a working on/off switch: it also turns the classic memes (Imgflip) on and off, so turning it off keeps MemeMe offline apart from the update check. When GIPHY refuses searches the item says why: "Key Not Accepted" (401/403; that key waits 10 min, a new key searches at once) or "Hourly Limit Reached" (MemeMe's own limit, or a 429, which pauses all searches for 10 min).
- The face is mapped on-device to one **mood word** (shocked, screaming, laughing, happy, smug, side eye, crying, sad, angry, confused, unimpressed, nervous, excited; with the hybrid matcher the emotion model's disgusted and scared too; the face details tongue out, wink, raised eyebrow, duck face, smirk, eye roll; or a pose's word). Of the face, only that word is sent, as one short tag-like phrase, since GIPHY matches tags and a longer sentence finds fewer, worse GIFs: "<mood> reaction" for the plain moods ("shocked reaction"), GIPHY's own tags as they are for the rest ("side eye", "unimpressed", "tongue out", "wink", "raised eyebrow", "duck face", "smirk", "eye roll", "mind blown", "t pose", "peace sign", "bowing"): `GET https://api.giphy.com/v1/gifs/search?api_key=…&q=<phrase>&limit=24&offset=0&rating=pg-13&lang=en`. Typed words go as `q=<typed words>` (see Search). A pose with no word of its own goes as the 1–3 words naming it ("flexing", "rock on"; see Capturing the face), made on the Mac by MemeMe's rules or by Apple's on-device model and normalized like typed words (lowercase, single-spaced, at most 50 characters, at least 3 letters or digits); the facts the model reads and the camera's frames never leave the Mac.
- **Where they show:** GIPHY's GIFs in For You's bottom row and in the GIFs tab (see Tabs); the classic memes in For You's top row, mixed with your things, and after your still images in the Memes tab. ↑ ↓ move between the two rows keeping the column (the last tile of a shorter row); an empty row is skipped. Without a GIPHY key there are no GIPHY GIFs. The search's word isn't repeated anywhere (it is beside the notch).
- **GIPHY's row:** 5 of GIPHY's first 24 results (10 in the GIFs tab without GIFs of yours) as square tiles, in GIPHY's order: the first ones, then, each time the same word is caught again, the next ones (wrapping around to the first after 24; with fewer, the page runs on into the first ones). One search fills several pages, so turning them costs no call. Tiles show GIPHY's small still (`fixed_width_small_still`) and play GIPHY's small preview (`fixed_width_small`) while focused. 1–9, the arrows, the pointer and ↵ work as on any tile. "More like this" is for your memes only. Before a face the row says "Make a face or a pose for GIFs from GIPHY." (unless your library is empty or no face can be read, with no camera, camera access denied or not yet given, or the camera in use: then it shows "reaction"); while searching (and while the on-device model names a pose) its tiles are placeholders; when GIPHY doesn't answer it says "GIPHY isn’t answering right now."
- **The classic memes:** picked 10 at a time, enough for both rows of the Memes tab; each tile shows the template whole (letterboxed), so a four-panel meme reads as one. Templates come from Imgflip's free list of the ~100 most popular (fetched at most once a day, kept in the cache folder, an older list used when Imgflip doesn't answer). MemeMe's own table (`MemeTemplateCatalog`) says what each famous template is for: its moods and the words people search it by ("guy looking back" → Distracted Boyfriend). A face's mood shows its templates first (those made for it, then those that also fit), then the most popular to fill the 10; the same word caught again shows the next 10 of that order, wrapping around; before a face, the most popular. All of it is picked on the Mac from MemeMe's own table: nothing is searched at Imgflip. Typed words show only templates that match them, by name, search words or a mood they name ("lol", "mad"), else "No classic memes for “…”." A pose with no word of its own shows the templates its words name (whole words, or the start of a word from 4 letters on; stopwords and body words such as "on", "hand" or "arm" never count, so "rock on" isn't The Rock), then its face's mood's, paged like a mood's, so the row is never empty. Nothing about the face, the pose or the words is sent to Imgflip, though the images of the templates picked load from Imgflip (so its servers see which templates were shown, as with any image). ↵ / click copy a template (as "reaction.png"), drag drops it ("meme.jpg"). While the list loads the tiles are placeholders; with no list at all: "Imgflip isn’t answering right now."
- **Speed.** The search starts as soon as the mood is clear, before the expression is held: once the expression being held has read as the same mood for 0.2 s (`EarlyMoodTrigger`). The hold takes 0.3 s for the first expression, 0.6 s for a new face and 1 s for a new gesture (0.3 s again after the face relaxed), so the row is usually filled the moment it completes. A pose with no word of its own is searched only once it is held, by its words (after the on-device model's answer, at most 2 s): its face's mood would spend a search on GIFs never shown. A mood's results are reused for 10 minutes. A search that fails on a dropped connection, a timeout or a server error is tried once more after 0.5 s (counted against the hourly limit).
- **Nothing GIPHY is stored.** Results live in memory for 10 minutes; images load from GIPHY's URLs through ordinary HTTP caching (as GIPHY's own apps do); the only files are full GIFs being copied or dragged, deleted within the hour. Sent GIPHY GIFs are not added to your library or to Recent (earlier versions did; those are removed at launch).
- Rate limit guard: at most 90 GIPHY calls per rolling hour per Mac. GIPHY counts calls per key, so every install of a build with a baked-in key shares that key's limit (100 per hour for a beta key): a public release needs a production key.
- The mark, under the tiles while GIPHY's GIFs show: "Powered by" (11 pt medium, muted) followed by GIPHY's logo, 12 pt tall: `Resources/GIPHY/GIPHYLogo.png` (white lettering and the coloured icon on transparent, for the black drawer), copied into the app by `scripts/build-app.sh`. Without the file it reads "Powered by GIPHY" in words. VoiceOver reads "Powered by GIPHY".

## Updates

MemeMe updates itself from GitHub Releases. Every change to the app on the default branch becomes a release (see README › Releases); the website's download link always points at the newest one.

- **Version:** `CFBundleShortVersionString` is the marketing version from the `VERSION` file (e.g. `1.0`); `CFBundleVersion` is the build number, the commit count of the default branch (e.g. `57`). Shown as **1.0 (57)**. Builds compare by build number only.
- **Feed:** `latest.json`, a release asset next to `MemeMe.dmg`, read from `https://github.com/<owner>/<repo>/releases/latest/download/latest.json` (Info.plist `MemeMeUpdateFeedURL`, written by `build-app.sh`). Builds without a feed URL (local builds, tests) never check and show no update UI.
- **When:** 20 s after launch, then every 6 hours while running, and after the Mac wakes if the last check is over an hour old. Silent: a failed check (offline, 404, bad JSON) changes nothing and shows nothing.
- **Telling the user (no system notifications, no permission prompt):**
  - The menu-bar icon gets a small dot in place of its top-right bracket (template image, so it follows the menu bar's color).
  - The panel footer shows one line when the panel opens, unless that build was dismissed: **“MemeMe 1.0 (58) is ready”** + muted **“ · <first release note>”** (when there is one), then **[Update]** (filled) and **[Not now]** (link). "Not now" hides the line for that build only; the dot and the menu item stay. Priority: indexing and the added-memes line win over it.
  - The menu starts with **Update to MemeMe 1.0 (58)…**.
- **Check for Updates…** (menu) runs a check right away and answers in an alert:
  - Up to date: **“You’re up to date”** / **“MemeMe 1.0 (57) is the newest version.”** [OK]
  - Available: **“MemeMe 1.0 (58) is available”** / up to 5 release notes as "• note" lines, a blank line, then **“You have 1.0 (57).”** [Update] [Later]
  - Needs a newer macOS: **“MemeMe 1.0 (58) needs macOS 15.0”** / **“Your copy keeps working. Update macOS to get the new version.”** [OK]
  - Failed: **“Couldn’t check for updates”** / **“Check your internet connection and try again.”** [OK]
- **Update (one click, from the footer, the menu item or the alert):** the panel closes; HUD **“Downloading MemeMe 1.0 (58)…”**; the DMG downloads to `~/Library/Caches/MemeMe/Updates/<build>/` and must match the manifest's size and SHA-256 and come from `github.com` or `*.githubusercontent.com` over HTTPS; when MemeMe itself is signed with a Developer ID, the DMG must also carry the same team's Developer ID signature (`codesign --verify --strict -R …`) before it is mounted or opened in Finder; it is mounted read-only; the app inside must have the same bundle id, exactly the manifest's build, and a valid code signature (`codesign --verify --deep --strict`) and, when MemeMe itself is signed with a Developer ID, the same team's Developer ID signature; it is copied next to the running app as a hidden staged bundle; HUD **“Restarting to finish the update…”**; MemeMe quits and a small detached script swaps the bundles and opens the new one. On the next launch: HUD **“Updated to MemeMe 1.0 (58)”** once.
- **When MemeMe can't replace itself** (it runs from a translocated or read-only location, or its folder isn't writable): it opens the downloaded DMG in Finder and says **“Finish updating in Finder”** / **“Drag MemeMe into your Applications folder and choose Replace, then open it again.”** [OK]. OK quits MemeMe, because Finder can't replace a running app. The same fallback covers mount, copy and relaunch failures. When MemeMe is signed with a Developer ID, Finder only ever gets a DMG that passed the team check.
- **Failures:** download failed → **“Couldn’t download the update”** / **“Check your internet connection, or download it from the website.”** [Open Download Page] [Cancel]. Verification failed (size, checksum, host, bundle id, build, or the app's or DMG's signature) → **“The update didn’t check out”** / **“MemeMe didn’t install it. Try again later, or download it from the website.”** [Open Download Page] [Cancel]; an unverified DMG is never opened. "Open Download Page" opens the release page (or the DMG URL when the manifest has no page).
- Releases are signed with a Developer ID and notarized, so updates keep camera and folder access; the first update from an older ad-hoc build asks once more.
